Introduction F2 Fintech is a loan origination company working with a network of 40+ banks and NBFCs. This guide brings together, in one place, the complete document checklist and step-by-step process flow for every loan product o ffered, so that both the internal team and the customer know exactly what is needed at every stage. 1.1 General Loan Process Flow (Applicable to All Products) While each product has its own specific paperwork, almost every loan journey at F2 Fintech follow s the same broad path: Lead Generation / Enquiry - customer shares basic requirement (loan type, amount, purpose). Login Creation - a case is opened on the F2 Fintech OMS with applicant's basic KYC & contact details. Eligibility Check - age, income/turnove r, CIBIL score and product-specific eligibility are screened against the applicable lender's policy. Document Collection - the applicant uploads the checklist of documents relevant to the product (see sections below). Login with Lender - the file is logged  in with the shortlisted bank/NBFC from our partner network. Underwriting & Verification - the lender's credit team verifies income, banking, KYC and (where applicable) property/business, along with telephonic or physical verification. Sanction Letter - th e lender issues an in-principle or final sanction with terms (rate, tenure, charges). Agreement & Post-Sanction Documentation - loan agreement, NACH/PDCs, mortgage/PTM (for secured loans) are executed. Disbursal - funds are disbursed to the applicant, seller, builder, or existing lender (in case of balance transfer). Note: Interest rates, processing fees, LTV and eligibility criteria shown in this guide are indicative and vary by lender, applicant profil e, CIBIL score, and ticket size. Terms & conditions apply. 2. Personal Loan An unsecured loan offered to salaried individuals working with corporates or government institutions, based on monthly salary - no collateral or security is required. 2.1 Process Flow Applicant profile check - age (21-60), minimum monthly salary (?25,000+), work experience (2 years+) and CIBIL score (685+) are validated. Login is created with applicant's KYC, employment and salary details. Complete document checklist (b elow) is collected and uploaded. File is logged in with the best-fit lender from the 40+ partner network based on salary/company category. Lender underwriting - banking analysis, employment/telephonic verification, and bureau check. Sanction letter issued  with approved amount, ROI and tenure (12-96 months depending on lender). Loan agreement signed and NACH/e-mandate set up for EMI. Disbursal directly to the applicant's bank account, typically within 2-5 working days. 2.2 Document Checklist - Salaried Appli cant # Required Document 1 PAN Card 2 Aadhaar Card 3 Form 16 (Last 2 Years) 4 ITR - Last 2 Years (if applicable) 5 Last 3 Months' Salary Slip 6 Last 6 Months' Bank Statement (salary account) 7 Official Email ID 8 Mother's Name 9 Company / Office ID Card 10 Passport-size Photograph 11 Ownership Proof or Rent Agreement (if rented) 12 Latest Utility Bill (address proof) 3. Business Loan Collateral-free funding for self-employed individuals and businesses (Proprietorship, Partnership, or Private Limited), based on business turnover/income rather than salary. 3.1 Process Flow Business profiling - entity type (Proprietorship / Partnership / Private Limited), business vintage (1-3 years+), turnover (?50 lakh+) and CIBIL (650+) are checked. Login created and entity type selected in the OMS drop-down. Entity-specific document check list is collected (see 3.2 below). GST,  Udyam  and banking data are cross-verified against declared turnover. File logged in with lender; underwriting covers ITR/financial analysis, banking, and business site verification. Sanction letter issued (unsecured  term loan, OD/CC limit, or secured option such as LAP/Machinery Loan). Documentation - loan agreement, PDCs/NACH, and (for secured options) mortgage/hypothecation papers. Disbursal to the current/business bank account, typically in 2-7 business days. 3.2 D ocument Checklist by Entity Type A. Pre-Approved Stage - a light-document, indicative offer is generated using PAN, Aadhaar, degree certificate, registration/COP-COM certificate and 6 months' banking . Applicant Type selection - Salaried or Self-Employed drop-down determines the additional underwriting documents required. Lender underwriting - income/banking verification, registration validation, and (for self-employed) ITR & Form 26AS review. Sanction letter issued - professionals typically enjoy no-collateral, no-income-proof (pre-approved) options with quick approval. Ag reement signing and NACH/PDC setup. Home Loan Financing for purchase of residential property - available as a Full Proposal (complete underwriting) or Short Proposal (lighter, faster track), across Fresh Purchase, Resale Purchase, Direct Builder Purchase and Balance Transfer. 5.1 Process Flow Proposal  Type selection - Full Proposal or Short Proposal (drop-down). Applicant Type selection - Salaried, Self-Employed Proprietor, or Self-Employed Partnership. Property Type selection - Fresh Purchase, Resale Purchase, Direct Builder Purchase, or Balance Trans fer (Balance Transfer available only under Salaried Full Proposal). Personal details captured - personal & official email, contact number, mother's name, current & office/business address, ID card photo. Loan documents collected as per the applicant + prop erty combination (see tables in 5.2 & 5.3). Property is legally vetted (title search) and technically valued by the lender's empanelled agencies. Credit underwriting - income/banking/ITR analysis plus property risk assessment. Sanction Letter issued along  with LOD (List of Documents) required for legal clearance. For Builder/Resale cases - Tripartite Agreement (TPA), PTM (Permission to Mortgage), or ATS (Agreement to Sell) executed as applicable. Disbursal - to seller (resale), builder (fresh/direct purchase), or existing lender (balance transfer). 5.2 Full Proposal - Document Checklist A. Salaried Applicant A1. Loan Against Property (LAP) A secured loan where an existing residential/commercial property is mortgaged to raise funds for business, personal or expansion needs - offered at attractive rates given the lower risk to the lender (up to 80% LTV). 6.1 Process Flow Applicant Type selecti on - Salaried or Self-Employed. Property valuation request raised; legal title search and technical inspection scheduled. Loan documents collected as per applicant profile (below). File logged in with lender; underwriting covers income/banking/ITR review p lus property legal & technical clearance. Sanction Letter issued along with LTV-based eligible amount. Mortgage creation - registration of mortgage / deposit of title deed as per state requirement. Loan agreement & NACH/PDC executed. Disbursal to the applicant's account (typically 10-15 working days). 6.2 Document Checklist - Salaried (Fresh & Resale Purchase / Property Owned) # Required Document 1 KYC (PAN & Aadhaar) of Applicant & Co-Applicant 2 Form 16 Part A & B (Last 2 Years) 3 Salary Slip (Last 3 Months) 4 Salary Account Bank Statement (Last 1 Year) 5 Property Documents (Title Deed / Ownership Papers) 6 Personal Email ID & Official Email ID 7 Contact Number & Mother's Name 8 Current Address & Office Address 6.3 Document Checklist - Self-Employed # Required Document 1 KYC (PAN & Aadhaar) of Applicant & Co-Applicant 2 GST Registration Certificate 3 Udyam  Registration Certificate 4 ITR with Computation (Last 3 Years) 5 Balance Sheet with CA-Certified Audit Report (Last 2 Years) 6 Form 26AS 7 Current Account Bank Statement (Last 1 Year) 8 Property Papers 9 Electricity Bill 10 Personal Email ID, Current & Permanent Address, Mother's Name, Business Office Address, Contact Number 7. Education Loan Financing for higher education (India or abroad), sanctioned jointly in the name of the Student and a Co-Applicant (parent/guardian) , based on the co-applicant's income and the admission secured by the student. 7.1 Process Flow Student's admission/offer letter and course details confirmed. Fee structure and entrance exam result (if applicable) reviewed to determine loan quantum. Studen t and Co-Applicant KYC and contact details captured. Co-Applicant's income documents collected (Salaried or Self-Employed) to assess repayment capacity. File logged in with lender; underwriting on co-applicant's income/banking and institute/course eligibil ity. Sanction letter issued, typically structured for disbursal in tranches aligned to fee due-dates. Loan agreement executed with student & co-applicant; NACH/PDC set up. Disbursal directly to the educational institute as per the fee schedule. 7.2 Document Checklist Student / Applicant # Required Document 1 Aadhaar Card 2 PAN Card 3 Class X, XII & Graduation Marksheets 4 Personal Email ID 5 Mobile Number 6 College/University Offer Letter 7 Fee Structure / Fee Schedule 8 Entrance Exam Result (if applicable) 9 Mother's Name Co-Applicant (Father / Mother / Guardian) # Required Document 1 Aadhaar Card 2 PAN Card 3 Email ID 4 Mother's Name 5 Last 6 Months' Bank Statement 6 Cancelled Cheque If Co-Applicant is Salaried - Additional Documents # Required Document 1 Form 16 Part A & B (Last 2 Years) 2 Last 3 Months' Salary Slip 3 Company / Govt.
